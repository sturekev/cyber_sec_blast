**QKD + Post-Quantum Cryptography: Building a Hybrid Secure Network**

## Description

How can networks prepare for a future where quantum computing challenges
today’s public-key cryptography?

This Computer Networks I research project explores Quantum Key Distribution
(QKD) and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) as parts of a hybrid network-security
architecture. It follows quantum-generated key material from the physical
channel, through key management and the ETSI GS QKD 014 API, into protocols
such as IKEv2.

The project also looks at the practical networking tradeoffs: QKD’s key rate
and distance limits, key-pool queuing and availability, trusted relay nodes,
and the need for an authenticated classical channel. The conclusion is not
that QKD replaces existing networks. Rather, PQC offers scalable protection
and authentication, while QKD may provide an additional source of key material
for selected high-value links.
